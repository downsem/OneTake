# OneTake Support

Need help with OneTake?

OneTake is a prompt-based social video app where users respond to prompts with short one-take videos and share them with friends.

## Contact

For help, account issues, safety concerns, privacy questions, or account deletion requests, contact:

## Account Help

If you are having trouble signing in, creating an account, updating your profile, or accessing prompts, contact support at the email above.

## Account Deletion

Users can delete their account directly in the app by going to:

**Profile → Delete Account**

Account deletion removes or anonymizes account data and related app records where applicable.

## Safety and Reporting

OneTake has zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive behavior. Users can report content or block users directly in the app. Reports are reviewed and acted on within 24 hours.

OneTake uses camera and microphone permissions to record video responses. Notifications are used to alert users when a new prompt is available. Permissions can be managed in iOS Settings.
