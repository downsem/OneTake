# OneTake Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** May 2026  
**App Name:** OneTake

## 1. Overview

OneTake provides a social video application where users respond to prompts with one-time video recordings and interact with friends.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share information when users use the app.

## 2. Information We Collect

### Account Information

When users create an account, we collect email address, username/display name, and a unique user ID.

### User Content

When users use the app, we may collect and store video responses, prompt responses, reactions, reports, blocks, and optional profile information.

User content is shared only with the selected audience inside the app, such as friends, subject to app functionality and safety controls.

### Device Permissions

With user permission, OneTake accesses camera, microphone, and notifications. Users can disable these permissions at any time in device settings.

### Usage and Analytics Data

We collect limited usage data such as app interactions, prompt activity, response activity, device type, timestamps, and technical information to improve reliability and user experience.

### Device and Notification Data

If notifications are enabled, we collect push notification tokens and basic device information to deliver app notifications.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use information to provide core app functionality, deliver prompts and notifications, enable friend-based interactions, improve app reliability, detect misuse, and support moderation and safety controls.

## 4. How We Share Information

We do not sell personal data.

Information may be shared with other users based on app functionality and visibility settings, with service providers such as Firebase/Google to operate the app, or when required by law or necessary to protect users and platform integrity.

## 5. Data Retention

We retain data while an account is active and as needed to provide services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce policies.

When users delete their account, account data is deleted or anonymized where applicable. Some data may remain temporarily in backups.

## 6. User Rights and Choices

Users can update account information, delete their account in the app, control permissions in device settings, and disable notifications.

## 7. Data Security

We use reasonable security measures, including secure cloud infrastructure, authentication controls, and restricted access. No system is completely secure.

## 8. Children’s Privacy

OneTake is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If such data is identified, it will be deleted.

## 9. Content and Moderation

Users are responsible for the content they create. OneTake may remove content, restrict accounts, review reports, and act on objectionable content or abusive behavior.
