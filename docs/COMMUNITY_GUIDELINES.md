# OneTake Community Guidelines

OneTake has zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users.

Users may not post or share content that includes harassment, bullying, hate, threats, sexual content, violence, spam, or content that targets, humiliates, exploits, or endangers another person.

Users can report objectionable content and block abusive users directly in the app. Blocking immediately removes the blocked user's content from the blocking user's feed and creates a developer moderation report.

OneTake will review objectionable content reports within 24 hours and may remove content or eject users who provided offending content.
